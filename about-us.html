About us

At Downtown Dental, everything we do is about connecting your lifestyle and goals with our foundation for oral health.

Health and wellness starts with making good lifestyle choices. At Downtown Dental we focus on the foundation of our patients’ oral health so that these choices can truly make a difference in their lives.

Conveniently located at the core of our vibrant city, our experienced team is proud to serve our patients with humour, professionalism, compassion and genuine care for their wellbeing.
