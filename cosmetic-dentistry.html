Cosmetic dentistry

Beauty begins within.

Beauty begins within from a sense of wellbeing that transcends physical appearance. Simply put, there is not a single aesthetic standard in which a person should fit. At Downtown Dental, we want you to feel good, not just look good.

Teeth whitening

Let your personality shine with a beautiful bright smile. From tooth whitening to full cosmetic procedures, at Downtown Dental we help your inner beauty shine.

Book a consultation

Invisalign®

If you are looking to obtain straighter teeth and a more beautiful smile without the look and inconvenience of traditional metal braces, Invisalign® clear aligners could be the perfect solution for you.

Learn more

Veneers & restorations

We install veneers, crowns, bridges, onlays and inlays depending on your unique dental needs, and only recommend procedures that are right for your teeth.

Learn more

Botox & Dysport

Our dentists are not only experienced at caring for your oral health, they are experienced with cosmetic treatments and have Botox training. Botox and Dysport treatments are administered by Dr. Whitney.

Learn more

Bite & jaw alignment

Straight teeth are great, but there’s a functional health benefit to having a proper bite and jaw alignment. It helps with airway health, blood oxygen levels and overall health!

Our philosophy

Redefining cosmetic dentistry, one mouth at a time.

We do not believe in “defects,” as they are a matter of interpretation. Instead, our main concern is with your oral health. The health of your teeth is connected to your entire wellbeing, and we know that if you feel good, you look good.
