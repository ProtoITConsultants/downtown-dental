Family dentistry

From infants to seniors.

Downtown Dental is pleased to be able to provide comprehensive dental services customized to each patient, no matter what age or stage of life. From infants and toddlers, to teens, adults and seniors, our dedication and commitment spans the full spectrum of dentistry.

With preventive care, education and effective treatment plans, the Downtown Dental team is committed to providing effective, affordable and quality dental and oral health care to you and your entire family. We look forward to helping you and every member of your family achieve optimal oral health.

We welcome families!

Believe it or not, even though we are located right on 9th Avenue in Downtown Calgary, many local Beltline, Eau Claire and Mount Royal families choose Downtown Dental as their family dentist of choice.

By providing easy, accessible, convenient and affordable dental care, you can be assured that you and your family can attain optimal oral health and wellness to last a lifetime! We take pride in ensuring that each visit is a stress-free and comfortable experience, with a commitment to providing you with personalized care and compassionate attention.

Here for you and your entire family

Downtown Dental focuses on educating all of our patients, no matter what age, on the importance of teeth cleaning practices as well as proper methods of cleaning. We know busy families will appreciate the ease and convenience of having Downtown Dental provide all your dental services in one convenient downtown Calgary location, rather than having to visit multiple dentists at different locations.

We focus on developing very strong connections with our patients, always emphasizing long-term patient relationships. By treating patients of all ages, we can provide a continuity of care as patients grow, fostering a familiar and comfortable environment, which is particularly helpful for young children and anxious patients.

If you have any questions about your oral health or any of the treatment options we provide, please contact us at (403) 264-1736 to set up your family dentistry appointment.
