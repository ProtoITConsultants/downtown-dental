General dentistry

Complete care for every age.

Although we are a downtown dental clinic, we welcome all ages. We think of our team as a family and encourage you to make our office your dental home for years to come.

01.

Examinations (adult and child)

Although we are a downtown dental clinic, we welcome all ages. We think of our team as a family and encourage you to make our office your dental home for years to come.

02.

Dental cleanings

Dental cleanings are the foundation to oral wellbeing. The Canadian Dental Association suggests that for optimal oral health, tartar and calcium buildup should be removed every three-to-six months. A thorough dental cleaning will also help us detect or diagnose oral problems from tooth decay to gum infections to oral cancers.

03.

Periodontal exams

Research demonstrates a connection between oral health and overall health. Poor oral health is linked to diabetes and heart disease among other ailments. We care about your overall wellbeing, shown in our thorough assessments of your bone and gum health.

04.

Root canals

Root canals refer to the process of removing the soft tissue inside a tooth, specifically when this tissue is infected and causing pain. We perform root canals in-office and will not refer you elsewhere unless it is absolutely necessary.

05.

Dental implants

Dental implants are a modern way of replacing a lost tooth and / or teeth. Learn about implants.

06.

Fixed restorations

We install veneers, crowns, bridges, onlays and inlays depending on your unique dental needs.

07.

Dentures

We offer partial dentures, complete dentures, and implant-supported dentures.

08.

Emergency dental treatment

If you have an emergency dental problem or require emergency treatment (tooth pain, abscess, emergency extractions, broken teeth, emergency root canal) our office will do everything we can to accommodate you as quickly as possible. Simply contact our office, explain your situation, and we will do our best to fit you in that same day.

09.

Night guards

A large portion of our population grind and/or clench their teeth while they sleep. This can result in multiple tooth problems including cracked or broken teeth, tooth pain, sensitive teeth and head, neck, muscle and joint pain (including morning headaches). Night guards are a protective mechanism for our natural teeth and dental restorations, muscles and joints. Learn about night guards.
