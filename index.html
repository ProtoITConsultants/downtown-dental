Our services

Tailored services,for every smile.

Our dentists understand your health goals and concerns, as well as how your teeth affect your overall health. Being a full service general dentistry clinic, we welcome all ages.

First impressions are powerful. We believe the new patient experience is the foundation for a healthy and happy relationship based on trust and mutual respect. We take our time and listen to your needs.

Although we are a downtown dental clinic, we welcome all ages. We think of our team as a family and encourage you to make our office your dental home for years to come.

Beauty begins within from a sense of wellbeing that transcends physical appearance. From tooth whitening to full cosmetic procedures, we help your inner beauty shine.

Our dentists value using mouth guards to protect an athlete’s teeth, mouth, gums, and possibly brain (from concussions through impact). Professional custom-made mouth guards provide optimal protection.

We can offer some oral surgical procedures within our dental facility, with some utilizing IV sedation. When it is beyond our expertise, we will assist you in referring out to a specialist.

About us

Everything we do is about connecting your lifestyle and goals with our foundation for oral health.

Health and wellness starts with making good lifestyle choices. At Downtown Dental we focus on the foundation of our patients’ oral health so that these choices can truly make a difference in their lives.

“At Downtown Dental, our dentists aim to create long-term relationships with our patients.”

Dr. Kristin Yont & Dr. Claire Whitney

  • Beautiful you.

    From tooth whitening to full cosmetic procedures, we help your inner beauty shine.

  • Confidence matters.

    Aligning the look and function of your teeth can go a long way towards restoring your confidence.

  • Not just cosmetic.

    A proper bite and jaw alignment helps with airway health, blood oxygen levels and overall health.
