About us

Everything we do is about connecting your lifestyle and goals with our foundation for oral health.

Health and wellness starts with making good lifestyle choices. At Downtown Dental we focus on the foundation of our patients’ oral health so that these choices can truly make a difference in their lives.

Our services

Tailored services.

Our dentists understand your health goals and concerns, as well as how your teeth affect your overall health. Being a full service general dentistry clinic, we welcome all ages.

  1. 01

    New Patient Experience

    First impressions are powerful. We believe the new patient experience is the foundation for a healthy and happy relationship based on trust and mutual respect. We accept all insurance.

  2. 02

    General Dentistry

    Although we are a downtown dental clinic, we welcome all ages. We think of our team as a family and encourage you to make our office your dental home for years to come.

  3. 03

    Cosmetic Dentistry

    Beauty begins within from a sense of wellbeing that transcends physical appearance. Simply put, there is not a single aesthetic standard in which a person should fit.

  4. 04

    Sports Dentistry

    Custom-made mouth guards protect an athlete’s teeth, mouth, gums, and possibly brain from concussions through impact. Professional mouth guards provide optimal protection.

  5. 05

    Dental Surgery

    We offer some oral surgical procedures within our dental facility, with some utilizing IV sedation. When it is beyond our expertise, we will assist you in referring out to a specialist.
