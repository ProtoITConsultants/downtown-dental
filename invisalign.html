What is Invisalign®?

Are you looking to have an amazing straight smile in Downtown Calgary? Downtown Dental offers affordable Invisalign® treatments. Your Invisalign® treatment will consist of a series of custom-made, clear plastic trays that are worn over your teeth to discreetly and gradually move your teeth into the desired position over time. The trays are individually created for each patient’s unique teeth, carefully designed to meet the smile goals agreed upon before commencing treatment.

The Invisalign® trays are secured in place by the contours of your teeth, as well as tiny tooth-coloured attachments that are affixed to certain teeth, exerting precise pressure for optimal alignment. The Invisalign® aligners are removable, so you can continue with your regular activities like drinking, eating and oral care.

The duration of Invisalign® treatment usually spans 9-15 months, with some people seeing improvement in shorter periods of time. In order to maximize the effectiveness of treatment, it is recommended that you wear your Invisalign® aligners 20-22 hours each day, changing to a new set every two weeks. Since the trays are transparent, Invisalign® is virtually undetectable, allowing discretion during treatment. When Invisalign® treatment is complete, the attachments are carefully removed, and you are left with straight teeth and the bright and beautiful smile you have always wanted.

What can Invisalign® treat?

Do you want to look amazing for that next board room or Zoom meeting? Downtown Dental offers Invisalign® so you can feel confident and look great at the same time. Invisalign® can provide effective treatment for a wide array of orthodontic issues for patients of all ages, including children, teens and adults. Those who have all of their adult teeth may be a candidate for Invisalign®.

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Crowding
  • Mild to moderate irregularities in the bite, such as overbites, open bites, underbites and crossbites

Downtown Dental is pleased to provide Invisalign® treatment as an effective solution to straighten your teeth and smile, or resolve mild to moderate orthodontic issues.
