Wisdom teeth

The last teeth to arrive.

Wisdom teeth are the last permanent teeth to erupt in the mouth, in the four corners at the back, top, and bottom. Wisdom teeth can appear without problems for many people, but for others, they appear with complications that require removal.

Wisdom teeth removal is a procedure offered by our experienced dentists at Downtown Dental in Calgary. Call us today to book your consultation.

Book a consultation

Do I need my wisdom teeth removed?

Your wisdom teeth tend to appear between the ages of 17 and 25. It’s common for wisdom teeth to erupt, causing problems in the mouth, such as:

  • Growing horizontally, at a right angle to your other teeth.
  • Growing at an angle, in any direction, causing problems with other teeth and making it difficult to clean.
  • Not erupting at all but staying beneath the jaw bone (also known as impacted wisdom teeth).

In all of these cases, your dentist will recommend removal to prevent further damage and discomfort in your mouth. In some cases, your dentist might recommend removal as a preventative measure. Our dental professionals will monitor the development of your wisdom teeth with dental X-rays during routine check-ups and keep you informed of any concerns.

What to expect during wisdom teeth extraction

Wisdom teeth extraction is a same-day procedure performed by our experienced dentists in our offices. The duration and complexity of the procedure will depend on the position of the teeth and the number of wisdom teeth being extracted.

At Downtown Dental, we offer local anesthesia and sedation options to keep you comfortable and pain-free. During your consultation, we will review the procedure, your treatment options and provide you with information about post-surgery care.
